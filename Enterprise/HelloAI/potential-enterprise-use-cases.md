# Potential Enterprise Use Cases for HelloAI

HelloAI can help employees ask plain-language questions about information in
CSV files. The following are potential use cases for teams working with
approved, regularly maintained data exports.

| Team | Example use | Potential benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Customer support | Look up product details, shipping estimates, or return-policy entries | Find answers faster without manually searching spreadsheets |
| Operations | Check order, delivery, or inventory exports | Speed up routine status checks and reduce manual filtering |
| Human resources | Find information in approved benefits or leave-policy tables | Make recurring policy lookups easier for employees |
| Procurement | Review supplier, price, or contract-summary exports | Compare supplier information more quickly |
| Sales | Look up product catalog, pricing, or territory data | Reduce time spent locating sales information |
| Finance | Review invoice, payment, or expense exports | Surface records and exceptions for finance staff to review |

These are ideas to validate with each team. Use only data that the organization
has approved for the application and its configured AI provider.
